Safe Disposal Makes a Big Difference

Learn the simple steps to dispose of hazardous waste safely and responsibly.

How to Dispose of Hazardous Waste?

Hazardous waste should never be thrown away with regular household waste. Proper disposal helps protect people, animals, soil, water and the environment.

Follow These Simple Steps

Responsible disposal starts with a few simple actions.

01
🔍

Identify the Waste

Check whether the waste contains harmful chemicals, electronics, medical materials or other hazardous substances.

02
📦

Keep It Separate

Keep hazardous waste away from regular household garbage to prevent contamination and accidents.

03
♻️

Use Approved Collection Centers

Take hazardous waste to authorized collection, recycling or treatment facilities.

04
📋

Follow Local Rules

Follow local waste-management guidelines and instructions provided by authorized authorities.

Dispose According to Waste Type

Different types of hazardous waste require different disposal methods.

E-Waste

Old phones, computers, batteries and electronic devices should be given to authorized e-waste collection or recycling centers.

Chemical Waste

Chemicals, paints and solvents should be handled through approved hazardous-waste collection facilities.

Biomedical Waste

Medical and healthcare waste should be disposed of through authorized biomedical-waste management systems.

Industrial Waste

Industrial hazardous waste must be managed using approved treatment, storage and disposal facilities.

Important Safety Tips

✓

Never mix hazardous waste with regular waste.

✓

Do not throw chemicals or batteries into drains.

✓

Use authorized collection and recycling centers.

✓

Follow local waste-management guidelines.

Not Sure How to Dispose of It?

Find the appropriate collection center or get guidance for safe hazardous-waste disposal.
